## [0.7.0] - UNRELEASED

v0.7.0 shows which application called the token, fixes system-wide
binding, and ships a container image.

What's new (one line each):

- App labels: `python3 [backup.py]`, `java [app.jar]` name the app behind the runtime. <!-- lane: app-identity -->
- Launcher chain: callers show `from backup.sh` (one hop; full chain in `-v`/JSON). <!-- lane: parent-chain -->
- `inventory --system` now binds usage on large hosts (was: zero rows). <!-- lane: invsys-zero -->
- stdout/stderr keep working under sudo with tighter capabilities. <!-- lane: caps-fix -->
- Container copy-ups attribute both file versions (`"layer": "pre_copy_up"` on the lower row). <!-- lane: copyup -->
- Tested against OpenSSL, p11-kit, pkcs11-tool and GnuTLS clients. <!-- lane: openssl-cells --> <!-- lane: p11kit-cov -->
- Container image `ghcr.io/mingulov/p11scope:0.7.0` for Docker and Kubernetes. <!-- lane: container-image -->
- Exact totals when many threads share one provider (rows may stay partial, see known issues). <!-- lane: mt-provider -->
- Tested on Linux 7.4-rc1, plus the 6.6–7.3 matrix. <!-- lane: k74 -->
- Measured cost: +4.7–+7.5 µs per call on SoftHSM; 970k calls/s strict-exact best ([numbers](../notes/2026-11-08-overhead-v070.md)). <!-- lane: perf-bench -->
- Docs pack: [troubleshooting](../troubleshooting.md), real first-run output, [why not just pkcs11-spy?](../overview.md#why-not-just-pkcs11-spy), [tested targets](../overview.md#tested-providers-and-applications).
